Hand and Foot Scorer

Track scores for the card game Hand and Foot across four rounds for two teams.

Books (7 cards of the same rank)

  • Red (clean): 500 pts
  • Black (dirty): 300 pts
  • Sevens: 5,000 pts
  • Fives: 3,000 pts
  • Wilds: 2,500 pts

Card Point Values

  • Jokers: 50 · Aces & 2s: 20 · 8–K: 10 · 4–7: 5

Penalties (cards left in hand/foot)

  • Black 3s: −100 each · Red 3s: −500 each
  • Remaining card points are subtracted

Bonuses

  • Going out: +100
  • Red 3s collected: +100 each
  • All 7 red 3s: extra +300

Meld Requirements

  • Round 1: 50 · Round 2: 90 · Round 3: 120 · Round 4: 150
