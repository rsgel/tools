Q3 Migration Runbook

Last edited 2 days ago · Owner: Priya N. · Shared with team

This runbook describes the steps for migrating the analytics pipeline to the new ingestion cluster. The cutover is scheduled for the last weekend of the quarter during the standard maintenance window.

Before starting, confirm that all dashboards have been snapshotted and that the on-call rotation has been notified. The rollback plan is documented in the appendix and should be rehearsed once in staging.

Throughput during the dry run held steady at roughly 1.2M events per minute with p99 latency under 400ms, which gives us comfortable headroom for the production volume.
