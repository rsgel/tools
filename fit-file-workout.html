Natural Language Syntax

Write one step per line. The parser understands:

  • Duration: 10 minutes, 3 miles, 5 km, 800m, lap button
  • Intensity: warmup, cooldown, rest, recover, easy, tempo, hard
  • Pace target: at 7:30 pace, @ 4:00/km
