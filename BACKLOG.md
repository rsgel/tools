# Tool Backlog

Ideas for new tools, roughly prioritized. Status: 🟢 Done | 🔵 In Progress | ⚪ Planned | 💡 Idea

## In Progress

- 🔵 **Pace Calculator** — Convert between pace/speed/finish time. Support mi/km toggle and all common race distances from 5K to 100mi. Show what a given pace means across distances.

## Planned

- ⚪ **Split Planner** — Enter a race distance + target time, get per-mile or per-aid-station splits. Support custom aid station distances and planned time-in-aid.
- ⚪ **Weekly Training Summary Formatter** — Paste or enter a week of activities, output clean markdown for sharing with Claude (total miles, elevation, time by sport, intensity).

## Ideas

- 💡 **Zone Calculator** — Input max HR or threshold HR, get training zones (5-zone and 7-zone). Running and cycling zones side by side.
- 💡 **Race Nutrition Planner** — Estimate cal/hour for a target effort, plan fueling (gels, drink mix, real food) per aid station or per hour.
- 💡 **Drop Bag Planner** — List aid stations, check off gear/nutrition per station, export as printable checklist.
- 💡 **Bike Commute Tracker** — Log daily commute rides, see weekly/monthly totals, estimate calories and CO₂ saved. localStorage-backed.
- 💡 **Elevation Gain Converter** — Convert ft/m, calculate vert-per-mile ratios, estimate time impact on pace.
- 💡 **Race Time Equivalency** — Enter a known result, estimate equivalent times at other distances using ultra-appropriate models.
- 💡 **TSS / Training Load Calculator** — Input duration + avg HR or power, estimate TSS. Track weekly load in localStorage.
- 💡 **Crew/Pacer Schedule Builder** — Enter course details + estimated splits, generate schedules for crew and pacers.
- 💡 **Gear Checklist Generator** — Select conditions (night, cold, rain, required gear), get a categorized packing list.
