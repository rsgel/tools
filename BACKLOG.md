## Planned

## Ideas

- 💡 **Crew/Pacer Schedule Builder** — Enter course details + estimated splits, generate schedules for crew and pacers.
- 💡 **Gear Checklist Generator** — Select conditions (night, cold, rain, required gear), get a categorized packing list.

## Garden Ideas (PNW Raised Bed)

- 💡 **PNW Planting Calendar** — Enter your USDA zone (8a/8b typical for PNW), see month-by-month timeline for when to start seeds indoors, transplant, and direct sow common veggies. Covers cool-season and warm-season crops with PNW-specific dates.
- 💡 **Raised Bed Planner** — 🟢 Done. Enter bed dimensions, drag-and-drop veggies into a grid layout using square-foot gardening spacing. Shows companion planting compatibility (e.g., tomatoes + basil 👍, tomatoes + brassicas 👎).
- 💡 **Seed Starting Calculator** — Pick your last frost date (typically mid-April in PNW lowlands) and select crops, get a countdown of exactly when to start each seed indoors and when to harden off / transplant.
- 💡 **Watering Schedule Helper** — Enter bed size, soil type, and current month. Estimates gallons per week needed, adjusting for PNW's dry summers vs. rainy spring/fall. Reminder that July–September is when raised beds really need consistent watering.
- 💡 **Soil Amendment Calculator** — Enter bed dimensions and depth, calculate cubic feet of compost/soil mix needed. Suggest PNW-friendly amendments (compost, worm castings, lime for acidic PNW soil, etc.).
- 💡 **Harvest Tracker** — Log what you pick and when. See totals by crop over the season. localStorage-backed. Fun for a first-year gardener to see what actually produced.
- 🟢 **Succession Planting Planner** — For crops like lettuce, radishes, and beans, calculate staggered sowing dates so you get continuous harvests instead of everything at once.
- 💡 **Days to Maturity Calculator** — Enter planting date and crop variety, see estimated harvest window. Helps figure out if you can still fit a crop in before PNW's early fall cool-down (late Sept).

## D&D Ideas

- 💡 **Encounter Builder** — Select party size and average level, get a CR budget. Browse monsters by CR, add them to the encounter, and see difficulty rating (easy/medium/hard/deadly) update live. Includes XP thresholds and adjusted XP for multiple monsters.
- � **Initiative Tracker** — Enter combatant names and initiative rolls, get a sorted turn order. Track current HP, conditions (stunned, prone, concentrating), and death saves. Advance turns with a button, highlights active combatant. localStorage to survive refreshes mid-combat.
- 💡 **Random NPC Generator** — One click generates a name, race, occupation, personality trait, ideal, bond, flaw, and a quirk. Optionally filter by race or setting. Copy as a formatted block for session notes.
- 💡 **Tavern Menu Generator** — Generate a random tavern with a name, specialty drink, menu items with prices (in cp/sp/gp), and a rumor the barkeep shares. Great for improv when the party inevitably goes to a tavern.
- 💡 **Loot Table Roller** — Select CR range and hoard vs. individual treasure. Roll on the DMG loot tables and display results: coins, gems, art objects, and magic items. Link magic item names to descriptions.
- 💡 **Spell Slot Tracker** — Pick a class and level, see your spell slot layout. Click to expend/recover slots. Track multiple casters in one view for the DM. Supports short rest (warlock) and long rest recovery. localStorage-backed.
- 💡 **Point Buy Calculator** — Interactive 5e point buy: adjust each ability score with +/- buttons, see points remaining, racial bonuses applied, and final modifiers. Warns when over budget. Export as a stat block.
- 💡 **Random Dungeon Room Generator** — Generate a room with dimensions, features (pillars, pools, rubble), a hazard or trap, possible monster, and treasure. Chain rooms together to improvise a dungeon crawl on the fly.
- 💡 **Wild Magic Surge Roller** — Roll on the Wild Magic Surge table (or a homebrew expanded version) with one click. Shows the effect with a dramatic reveal animation. Includes a d20 "did I surge?" pre-roll.
- 💡 **Session Notes Template Generator** — Enter session number, date, party members present, and location. Generates a structured markdown template with sections for recap, key events, NPCs met, loot gained, and open threads. Copy to clipboard.
- 💡 **Travel Time Calculator** — Enter distance in miles, party travel pace (slow/normal/fast), and terrain type (road, forest, mountain, swamp). Calculates days of travel, miles per day, stealth/perception modifiers, and forced march exhaustion risk.
- 💡 **Name Generator by Culture** — Generate fantasy names filtered by cultural flavor: elvish, dwarvish, orcish, infernal, draconic, etc. Generates first name, surname, and an optional epithet. Bulk generate 10 at a time for worldbuilding.
