Scoring (penalty points for cards left in hand)
  • Number cards 1–9: 5 pts each
  • Number cards 10–12: 10 pts each
  • Skip card: 15 pts each
  • Wild card: 25 pts each
Winning

First player to complete Phase 10 wins. If multiple players finish in the same round, the lowest score wins.
